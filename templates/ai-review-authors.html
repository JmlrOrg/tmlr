AI Reviews at TMLR

Information for authors

As announced in the TMLR blog, TMLR will be conducting a research study where all TMLR submissions will receive an additional review automatically generated by an AI reviewer. The goal of this experiment would be to assess whether AI reviewers can generate reviews of sufficient quality to be valuable to the author and/or the review process. Note that the AI review only addresses the question of whether the claims in the paper are met with accurate and convincing evidence.

  • Your submission will receive one review automatically generated by an AI reviewer in addition to conventional reviews.
  • You will be told which review is AI-generated.
  • You will have a chance to respond to the human-written reviews as well as the AI-generated review.
  • There will be a discussion period as per the normal protocol with the human reviewers.
  • The Action Editor will make a decision based on all available information.
  • When the recommendation stage begins, i.e., after the discussion period, you will be invited to complete a short research survey about the AI-generated review. Participation in that survey is voluntary and will not affect the outcome or handling of your paper in any way.

This experiment aims to improve TMLR’s understanding of how AI-generated reviews might support the peer-review process in the future. We appreciate your contribution to this initiative and your support in advancing transparent and rigorous reviewing practices.

As noted in this blog post, we are including one review generated by an AI reviewer alongside the usual human-written reviews with the goal of understanding whether AI-generated reviews are of sufficient quality to be used in the review process. We are conducting a brief research study to understand how authors, reviewers, and editors perceive the quality and usefulness of these reviews.

You are invited to complete a short survey about the AI-generated review on your paper. Please note that participation in this survey is entirely optional. Choosing not to participate will not affect your role, the review process, or the decision on the manuscript. Your responses to this survey will also not affect the evaluation or decision of the manuscript. There will be no compensation for participating in this survey. Any reported findings will be anonymized and will not be linked to you or the manuscript in public outputs. Completing the survey will take approximately 5-10 minutes. The survey will be open until the decision for the paper is completed.

To be eligible to participate in the survey, you must be at least 18 years of age. If you agree to participate, please click on the "Survey" button below the AI review. By clicking the "Survey" button, you are confirming that you are at least 18, that you have read and understand this information, and that you agree to participate in the survey.

If you have any questions about the study, please contact Nihar B. Shah at nihars@cs.cmu.edu.

Thank you for considering this invitation and for helping us improve the quality and transparency of peer review at TMLR.

Sincerely,
