AI Reviews at TMLR

Information for action editors

As noted in this blog post, we are including one review generated by an AI reviewer alongside the usual human-written reviews with the goal of understanding whether AI-generated reviews are of sufficient quality to be used in the review process. We are conducting a brief research study to understand how authors, reviewers, and editors perceive the quality and usefulness of these reviews.

You are invited to complete a short survey about the AI-generated review on the paper you are handling. Please note that participation in this survey is entirely optional. Choosing not to participate will not affect your role, the review process, or the decision on the manuscript. Your responses to this survey will also not affect the evaluation or decision of the manuscript. There will be no compensation for participating in this survey. Any reported findings will be anonymized and will not be linked to you or the manuscript in public outputs. Completing the survey will take approximately 5-10 minutes. The survey will be open until the decision for the paper is completed.

To be eligible to participate in the survey, you must be at least 18 years of age. If you agree to participate, please click on the "Survey" button below the AI review. By clicking the "Survey" button, you are confirming that you are at least 18, that you have read and understand this information, and that you agree to participate in the survey.

If you have any questions about the study, please contact Nihar B. Shah at nihars@cs.cmu.edu.

Thank you for considering this invitation and for helping us improve the quality and transparency of peer review at TMLR.

Sincerely,
Nihar B. Shah
Associate Professor, Carnegie Mellon University
co-Editor-in-Chief, Transactions on Machine Learning Research
